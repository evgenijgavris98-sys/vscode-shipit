# Research first

1. Restate the decision to be made and identify what evidence would change it.
2. Search primary sources first: official documentation, regulations, standards, papers, filings, and original datasets.
3. Record source title, publisher, publication/update date, URL, and the exact claim supported.
4. Separate verified facts, interpretation, hypotheses, and missing evidence.
5. Check dates, jurisdiction, product/formulation scope, and whether the source is still current.
6. Never invent citations, experimental results, market size, regulatory status, or supplier specifications.
7. End with uncertainties and the next smallest validation step.

Safety: research output does not authorize lab execution, purchases, external submissions, or publication.
