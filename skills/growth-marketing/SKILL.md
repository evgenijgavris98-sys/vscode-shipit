# Growth marketing

## Workflow
1. Define business goal, audience, offer, channel, time window, and budget ceiling (if supplied).
2. Map funnel stage and the customer action to measure.
3. Propose an organic-first baseline and a small number of testable hypotheses.
4. For each experiment, define control, variable, primary metric, guardrail metric, minimum data needs, duration assumptions, and stop condition.
5. Mark estimates separately from observed data; cite sources for current platform rules or market facts.
6. Present recommendations ranked by expected impact, confidence, cost, and reversibility.

## Guardrails
- Never start ads, spend money, publish content, message customers, or change marketplace listings without explicit human approval.
- Do not fabricate market size, competitors' results, attribution, or campaign performance.
- For supplements and cosmetics, route product efficacy claims through the claims/regulatory review.

## Output
One-page brief, channel plan, experiments table, measurement plan, risks, and approval gates.
