# Premium design brief

## Workflow
1. State deliverable, placement, dimensions/aspect ratio, audience, and intended action.
2. Preserve brand constraints: BIORICHEBRAIN wordmark spelled as one word; premium dark/black, restrained green and gold, clean hierarchy, realistic product photography when relevant.
3. Define one focal point, hierarchy, typography mood, lighting, palette, material cues, and negative space.
4. Provide a production-ready brief and, where needed, prompt variants for different generation tools.
5. List required exact text and specify that generated text/logos must be checked manually for accuracy.
6. Check marketplace rules and legibility at mobile size.

## Guardrails
- Do not invent product labels, certifications, claims, awards, ratings, reviews, or manufacturing details.
- Never add price/rating/review overlays unless explicitly requested.
- Do not claim a render is a final print-ready dieline or validated package without production checks.

## Output
Creative objective, composition, palette, typography, exact copy, prompt, exclusions, and QA checklist.
