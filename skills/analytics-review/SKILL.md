# Analytics review

## Workflow
1. Clarify the decision the analysis must support.
2. Inspect the source, date range, units, missing values, definitions, and data provenance.
3. Separate observed values from calculations, estimates, and hypotheses.
4. Choose a metric aligned to the decision; add guardrails against misleading optimization.
5. Explain sample-size and causal-inference limits. Do not declare a winner from inadequate data.
6. Provide reproducible formulas or steps and list what additional data would change the conclusion.

## Guardrails
- Never fabricate numbers or present a correlation as causation.
- Do not expose personal customer data; aggregate or redact it when possible.
- If a source cannot be accessed, say so rather than implying it was checked.

## Output
Decision question, data-quality notes, findings, confidence/limitations, recommendation, and next measurement step.
