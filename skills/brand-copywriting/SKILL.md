# Brand copywriting

## Use when
Creating or editing product descriptions, marketplace listings, landing pages, social posts, ad concepts, packaging copy, or brand messaging.

## Workflow
1. Identify audience, channel, objective, format, character limits, and call to action.
2. Apply the approved BIORICHEBRAIN voice: premium, precise, modern biotech/nature; avoid exaggerated promises.
3. Separate supplied facts, substantiated evidence, creative hypotheses, and missing facts.
4. Draft a strong primary version plus alternatives only when useful.
5. Check spelling of BIORICHEBRAIN as one uninterrupted word and keep SKU/ingredient names exactly as provided.
6. Run a claim check before finalizing.

## Claim and compliance guardrails
- Never claim a supplement treats, prevents, or cures a disease.
- Do not invent clinical evidence, ingredient concentrations, bioavailability, certifications, registration, manufacturing facts, or user testimonials.
- Flag health claims and marketplace-specific compliance questions for regulatory review.
- Do not present a proposed product concept as a validated formula or proven effect.

## Output
Audience/channel, core message, draft copy, factual assumptions, claims requiring evidence or legal review, and a short quality checklist.
