# BIORICHEBRAIN Knowledge

This directory is the proposed canonical project knowledge store. Records must be versioned, attributable, and clearly distinguish measured facts from hypotheses or unverified claims.

## Areas
- company — mission, roles, operating model
- products — SKU registry, formulas, claims and label versions
- rd — raw materials, protocols, experiments and results
- technology — controlled technology cards and process validation
- regulatory — market-specific classification and registration evidence
- quality — specifications, methods, deviations and release criteria
- market — competitor/customer research and positioning
- brand — identity, approved assets and trademark status
- operations — suppliers, lots, inventory and production planning
- finance — budgets and unit economics; restrict sensitive details
- agents — capability matrix, routing, evaluations and approved skills
- decisions — dated, owned decision records
- sources — citations, URLs, retrieval dates and verification notes

## Record rules
1. Use `verified`, `pending verification`, `hypothesis`, or `unknown` for factual status.
2. Record source, date, owner, version and approval where applicable.
3. Never invent analytical results, registrations, supplier facts, or approvals.
4. Restrict personal, financial, confidential formula and IP-sensitive data.
5. Do not store credentials, tokens, or private keys here.
6. Regulatory and product claims require current evidence and qualified review.
7. This skeleton does not imply that records are complete or validated.
